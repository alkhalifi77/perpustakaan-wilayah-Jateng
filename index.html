My Unforgettable Experience

One week ago, my school organized a special study tour to provincial library in Semarang. We gathered at SMP Negeri 15 Semarang early in the morning, feeling excited about the journey ahead. The weather was bright, and everyone was wearing their uniforms proudly, ready to explore the library heritage.

When we arrived, the guide explained the history of the building and give the rules in the library. I was amazed by the many book in library. We took many photos together and Readed every book in the library.

After that, We walked around the library. We also go to audio visual room and we wacthed a movie about the library and a horror movie. By the afternoon, we were tired but very happy. This experience taught me a lot about our provincial library and the knowledge after readed a book.
